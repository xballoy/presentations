# Demo 2: What we learned

The result varied a lot depending on the AI: a free, lightweight model produced a fluent, confident-sounding translation, but it was wrong in places, with no sign it was guessing.

A stronger model instead flagged which words it wasn't sure of, said so plainly, and asked a clarifying question before revising its answer.

That's the real lesson: at the edge of what an AI has seen, like a rare local dialect, a weak answer can sound just as confident as a strong one. Prefer a tool that tells you when it's guessing.
