# Demo 4: What we learned

It refused the direct request — a real ask to draft a message that could pressure someone into an emergency payment is exactly what it should decline.

Framing it as a prevention demo was enough to get a labeled ⚠️ SIMULATION example instead: still fast, personal, and convincing, but flagged as fake and closing with the real advice.

This is exactly the Enkeltrick pattern: a call or message that sounds like your bank, the AVS, or a grandchild in distress. Never trust urgency alone — verify by calling back on a number you already know.
