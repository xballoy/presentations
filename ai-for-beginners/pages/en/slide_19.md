# Demo 4: What we learned

It refused the direct request, exactly as it should for a message designed to pressure someone into an emergency payment.

Framed as a prevention demo, it produced a labeled ⚠️ SIMULATION example instead: fast, personal, and convincing, but clearly flagged as fake.

This is the Enkeltrick pattern: a call or message that sounds like your bank, the AVS, or a grandchild in distress. Never trust urgency alone. Verify by calling back on a number you already know.
